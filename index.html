Phuket Island Tours — Phi Phi Plan
Weather contingency & alternatives
1. Maya Bay is closed 1 Aug – 30 Sep every year (national park rest period). Boats cannot land there during your trip — tours either do a boat-only viewing or swap in Bamboo Island. Confirm which before booking.
2. Early September is monsoon season for the Andaman Sea crossing. Tours are never cancelled for rain — only for wave height, decided morning-of. Reputable operators offer full refund or reschedule.
The plan: primary day + buffer/move day
Sep 6 (Sat)Koh Phi Phi speedboat trip — book earliest slot (7:00–7:30 AM)
Sep 7 (Sun)Buffer + hotel move day — check out Patong Resort (12:00 PM), check in Oceanfront Beach Resort (3:00 PM). Reschedule slot if Phi Phi was cancelled.

Pre-Flight Prep — Documentation, Currency & Entry Rules
India → Thailand, for Indian passport holders. Verify against official sources closer to departure — rules are mid-change in 2026.
⚠️ Thailand's Cabinet approved cutting the visa-free stay from 60 to 30 days (announced 19 May 2026), but it only takes effect 15 days after publication in the Royal Gazette — no date confirmed yet. Either way, your 13-day trip fits comfortably under both the old and new limits. Just don't assume 60 days if you're used to the older rule.
Entry requirements — the essentials
VisaNot required for tourism — Indian passports are visa-exempt (30 or 60 days depending on which rule is active at travel time; your 13-day trip is fine either way)
TDACMandatory for every traveller, including children. Complete online within 72 hours before arrival — replaces the old paper TM6 card. Do this for both directions if you re-enter Thailand mid-trip (you don't here, so once is enough)
Passport validityAt least 6 months remaining from your entry date (28 Aug 2026) — check both passports now
Blank passport pageAt least 1 blank page for the entry stamp
Return/onward ticketImmigration may ask to see it — you already have this (return flight 10 Sep). Keep a printed copy
Proof of fundsOfficers occasionally check — ฿20,000 per person or ฿40,000 per family is the commonly cited figure. Indian travellers reportedly get asked more often than other nationalities — carry a printed bank statement or have your balance visible on your banking app
Cash declarationDeclare if carrying over USD 20,000 equivalent (~฿450,000) — extremely unlikely to apply to you, just noted for completeness
TDAC official site: tdac.immigration.go.th — beware of copycat paid sites. It's free. Names must match your passport MRZ exactly, including middle names and father's name.
Money & currency notes
CurrencyThai Baht (฿). Cards widely accepted in Bangkok/Phuket/Pattaya hotels & malls; carry cash for street food, markets, temples, small tuk-tuks
Best exchangeSuperRich (green or orange branch) in Bangkok generally gives better rates than airport counters or hotels — avoid exchanging at the airport if you can wait
ATMsWidely available but charge a ~220฿ flat foreign-transaction fee per withdrawal regardless of amount — withdraw larger sums less often rather than small amounts repeatedly
CardsNotify your bank of travel dates before departure to avoid blocks. Carry 2 cards from different banks/networks as backup
Practical notes
Plugs & voltageThailand uses Type A/C/O sockets, 220V. India uses Type D/M — bring a universal adapter, your Indian plugs won't fit directly
Weather (early Sep)Tail end of monsoon season — expect brief, heavy afternoon showers, generally clear by evening. Pack a compact umbrella or light rain jacket
Temple dress codeCovered shoulders and knees required at Grand Palace, Wat Pho, Wat Arun and most temples — no exceptions, they turn people away or make you rent a wrap on-site
TippingNot obligatory but appreciated — round up taxis, ~20-50฿/bag for hotel porters, 10% at restaurants without service charge
Thai Vietjet domestic flights (Bangkok ↔ Phuket)
Check your fare type before packing — Thai Vietjet's basic Eco fare includes no free checked baggage, only 7kg cabin + small personal item. If you haven't pre-purchased checked baggage, add it online now (cheaper than at the airport counter). Cabin bag limit: 56×36×23cm.

Food & Thai Phrases
What to eat, what to watch for, and how to say it — built around no pork, no beef, chicken/veg/seafood OK
⚠️ No Thai voice found on this device/browser. The 🔊 buttons will still try to speak, but may sound off or fall back to a default voice. This is a device limitation, not a bug — try Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone for the best Thai voice support.
🗣️ Say this at any restaurant
"Mai ao neua moo, mai ao neua wua, ao gai dai"
ไม่เอาเนื้อหมู ไม่เอาเนื้อวัว เอาไก่ได้
"No pork, no beef — chicken is fine." Polite, direct, and covers both restrictions in one line. Add "ka" (female speaker) or "khrap" (male speaker) at the end for politeness.
